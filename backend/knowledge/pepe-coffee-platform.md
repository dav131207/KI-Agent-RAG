# pepe.coffee: what the platform is
pepe.coffee is a free, non-custodial tipping platform for the Pepecoin
(PEP) community, similar in spirit to Linktree or Buy Me a Coffee.
Anyone can create a personal page at pepe.coffee/yourname with links, a
bio, and a tip button. Payments go directly wallet-to-wallet on the
Pepecoin blockchain; pepe.coffee never holds anyone's PEP. The public
site is privacy-focused (no real name or ID required, email optional)
and is English-only. pepe.coffee considers itself part of the
r/pepecoin community and has no association with Matt Furie, the
creator of Pepe the Frog.

## pepe.coffee: creator pages
On pepe.coffee, each creator page has links, a bio, design themes, and
a tip button where tippers can add their name and a message. A
creator can also set a "Goal", a free fundraising target shown on
their own page. Pages come with sharing tools: a QR code, an
embeddable tip button, a tip card, a forum badge, and a GitHub banner.
New pages only appear in pepe.coffee's "Explore" and "Meet the
creators" listings after manual admin approval. A blue checkmark next
to a creator's name means an admin has verified that specific page.

## pepe.coffee: Projects
On pepe.coffee, "Projects" let any account holder pitch a community
project with no voting involved. A one-time fee of 500 PEP makes a
project go live immediately and earns it a green "Supporter" badge.
Only paid, live projects are shown on the pepe.coffee homepage under
"Live in the community" — the fee is what buys that visibility, not a
popularity vote.

## pepe.coffee: streamer program
pepe.coffee's streamer program pays streamers with at least 500
followers (Twitch, TikTok, YouTube, or Kick) a one-time reward worth
10 euro in PEP, for streamers who stream at least 3 times in 30 days
and show the pepe.coffee overlay, labeled as advertising. It is
limited to the first 50 streamers. The tagline used to promote it is
"Streaming GTA? Get paid in Pepecoin." Payouts are currently handled
manually by a pepe.coffee admin, not automatically.

## pepe.coffee: paper wallets
pepe.coffee's paper wallet tool (under /tools/) produces printable
sticker sheets with a QR code and a paper wallet, with private keys
generated in the visitor's own browser. Two comic-style designs exist
(Christmas and Matrix themes), in all supported languages. To redeem
one, open it in a wallet app such as Coinomi and choose "Sweep
wallet" — the same steps work on iPhone and Android. The wallets hold
only fractions of a cent on purpose, so there are deliberately few
warning screens.

## pepe.coffee: built-in wallet
pepe.coffee has a built-in, password-protected web wallet, with its
own password separate from a creator's page-login password. This
wallet automatically recognizes Pepecoin NFTs (inscriptions) held in
it and protects them: NFT-holding outputs are never spent by accident
as a regular payment or as network fee/change. NFTs can be viewed,
held, and sent directly from this built-in wallet, with links out to
the Pepeblocks explorer and to Nintondo for trading.

## pepe.coffee: languages
pepe.coffee's main site is available in 12 languages: English plus
Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean,
Italian, French, Indonesian, Vietnamese, and simplified Chinese. Only
the site's own interface is translated — a creator's own bio, links,
and project pitches stay in whichever language they were written in.
The admin area, imprint, and design editors remain English-only. A
visitor's browser language is only auto-detected on their very first
visit; after that, their own chosen language is remembered.

## pepe.coffee: non-custodial and pseudonymous, not anonymous
pepe.coffee never holds user funds; every payment moves peer-to-peer
on the public Pepecoin blockchain. Because that blockchain is public,
"anonymous" on pepe.coffee really means pseudonymous, not untraceable.
pepe.coffee does not verify anyone's real-world identity — a blue
checkmark on a creator page only means a pepe.coffee admin manually
reviewed and approved that one page, not that the person behind it
has been identity-checked.

## pepe.coffee: features not live yet
As of November 2026, these pepe.coffee ideas are not live yet and
should not be described as available: a public NFT gallery on
creator pages with ownership verification, badges or perks unlocked
by owning specific NFTs, a public airdrop signup/landing page, fully
automated streamer payouts, a simple built-in wallet without a seed
phrase, in-site browser games, and a blog or news section. These are
either in progress or still just ideas.
