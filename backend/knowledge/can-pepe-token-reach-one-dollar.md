# Can $PEPE (the Ethereum meme token) ever reach $1?
Community explanation, September 2026: probably not, short of
hyperinflation making the dollar worthless, and then everyone has bigger
problems. Price times supply is market cap, and $PEPE's supply makes a $1
price arithmetically out of reach. The sections below run the numbers.

## The supply math behind a $1 $PEPE
Community explanation, September 2026: $PEPE, the Ethereum meme token, has
a supply of about 420 trillion tokens. At $1 per token its market cap
would have to be 420 trillion USD. For reference, Bitcoin's market cap is
around 1.5 trillion USD, roughly half of all crypto combined. The
estimated total global wealth is about 470 trillion USD, so almost
everyone on the planet would have to put all their money and assets into
$PEPE. That is not going to happen.

## How to sanity-check any price target
The same check works for any coin, including Pepecoin (PEP): multiply the
hoped-for price by the circulating supply and compare the result with the
market caps of the largest assets in the world. If the number lands above
Bitcoin, or above whole asset classes, the target is a fantasy. Whether a
coin goes up a little is anyone's guess; Professor Pepe does not predict
prices. Do your own research.
