# Why Pepecoin (PEP): what the community sees in it
Community explanation, September 2026, from a holder who is new to crypto
themselves: the case for Pepecoin (PEP) is not random hype. It rests on a
fair launch, merged mining with Dogecoin and Litecoin, near-zero fees, a
Layer 1 wallet nobody can freeze, its own infrastructure, and a community
that has proven itself under pressure. The sections below go one by one.

## Fair launch: every PEP was mined by someone
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): PEP is a fair-launch coin. Unlike
Ethereum tokens, where the developers can hand themselves huge amounts of
the supply on day one to dump for profit, every PEP in existence was mined
by someone. There was no premine, no ICO and no insider allocation, which
is what gives the coin real value in the community's eyes.

## Merged mining with Dogecoin and Litecoin, and the 51% attack question
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): PEP is a Layer 1 coin that is
merge-mined together with Dogecoin and Litecoin, so the same Scrypt work
can earn all three at once. This helps security a lot: to hurt the chain
with a 51% attack, an attacker would need to beat the combined mining
power of Litecoin, Dogecoin and Pepecoin together, not just Pepecoin's.

## Near-zero fees and tipping
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): as a Layer 1 coin, moving PEP
costs basically nothing, less than a cent. On Ethereum a simple transfer
to another wallet or an exchange can cost around 5 USD. The difference is
what makes it possible to spread PEP everywhere and tip people, which the
community does all the time on its Discord server.

## The community: Dogecoin 2014 vibes
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): the community is a big part of
the appeal. People who were around in 2014 say it feels like early
Dogecoin, and the Discord is a fun place to chat. Newcomers to crypto are
welcome and common.

## The Xeggex rug pull, and how the community handled it
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): the community proved itself when
Xeggex, at the time PEP's major exchange, was rug-pulled by its owner.
Some community members organised and managed to get the community's
funds back. It is told as a story of resilience, not of the exchange:
PEP itself was never at risk, only the funds held on Xeggex.

## Wallets cannot be frozen
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): a PEP wallet cannot be frozen or
blacklisted. Ethereum tokens can carry a contract owner who blocks
wallets, sometimes to stop holders from selling for a profit. PEP is a
native Layer 1 coin with no contract and no owner, so no such switch
exists.

## Own infrastructure: Pepdle and other projects
Community explanation of Pepecoin (PEP): the community builds its own
infrastructure rather than relying on third parties. One example is
Pepdle, a Wordle-style game that pays out PEP for guessing the daily word.
There is also a team working behind the scenes on the chain and its
tooling.
