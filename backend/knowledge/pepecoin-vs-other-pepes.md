# Pepecoin (PEP) versus the other "Pepe" coins
Community explanation, September 2026. Three different projects share the
Pepe name and get mixed up constantly: the 2016 Pepecoin (now an Ethereum
token), the $PEPE meme token on Ethereum, and Pepecoin (PEP), the
merge-mined Scrypt Layer 1. The sections below tell them apart.

## The 2016 Pepecoin (now an Ethereum token)
Community explanation of the 2016 Pepecoin, not to be confused with
Pepecoin (PEP): it started as its own Layer 1 chain, but with a premine
and a dev fee. It later moved to Ethereum because it lacked the miners to
keep its chain secure, and on the move the team minted an extra 66% of the
supply, so every holder's stake was worth a lot less overnight. With
Ethereum's gas fees it cannot do things the PEP community enjoys, like
tipping each other.

## $PEPE, the Ethereum meme token
Community explanation of $PEPE, the Ethereum meme token, not to be confused
with Pepecoin (PEP): it launched with massive dev pre-allocations on day
one (up to 30% of supply). It already made its creators rich, and with a
market cap in the billions there is little realistic room for the returns
you get from finding a newer project early.

## $PEPE contract risk: the owner can blacklist wallets
Community explanation of a risk in $PEPE, the Ethereum meme token: the
token contract lets the owner blacklist a wallet and stop it from
interacting with the contract. One holder had a wallet worth about 70
million USD at the all-time high that was blocked, so they could not do
anything with it. Pepecoin (PEP) is a native Layer 1 coin with no token
contract and no owner, so nobody can block a PEP wallet.

## Pepecoin (PEP), the Scrypt Layer 1
Pepecoin (PEP), as the community describes it, does things the fair way: a
100% fair launch, no premine, no ICO, no insider allocation, and low fees.
It is one of the most secure networks in crypto thanks to merged mining
with Dogecoin and Litecoin, which means it shares the same physical Scrypt
miner base. It is still a small project compared to the big meme tokens.
The goal is to build THE Pepe community, where everyone is welcome.
